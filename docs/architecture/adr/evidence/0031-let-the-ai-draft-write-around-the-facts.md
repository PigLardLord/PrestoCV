# Evidence for ADR 0031: Let the AI draft write around the facts

The measurements behind [ADR 0031](../0031-let-the-ai-draft-write-around-the-facts.md). Every input was invented: no person's data was used. The thresholds they are read against are in the ADR's [The measurement](../0031-let-the-ai-draft-write-around-the-facts.md#the-measurement). The ADR says what was decided and why; this file keeps the numbers it rests on, as they were measured.

**Measured: NO-GO at revision b14ceda8** (macOS 27.0.1 build 26A434, Xcode 27.1, 30 inputs and the language-data input of the app, 1 run each; the model is deterministic, so the 3 runs of an input are identical, as the 90-run set at a1e64ab5 showed). Against 6fc5daab (the previous measurement):
- Invented facts: 0 (0). Failed runs: 0 (2). Missed examples: 0 (0). Tools of another trade: 0 (0). One fact lost, a job title the description does not give.
- A summary in 22 of 25 distinct drafts of inputs with a role, study or field (16), and in 6 of the 6 examples (3). Missing: `en-traps` (115 characters in 2 sentences), `it-traps` (the quote of the description, 68 characters), `ai-language-data` (22 characters). Threshold 90%: not met.
- A description on 33 of 37 kept jobs (19 of 38). Missing: both jobs of `it-traps`, the second job of `example-es` (the quote only), the job of `ai-language-data`. Threshold 90%: not met.
- Enough technologies and soft skills in 23 of 25 drafts (14). Missing: `fr-traps` (3 technologies), `ai-language-data`. Met.
- Every written sentence in the description's language: 158 of 158. Not seen by that count: the nine skills of `it-traps` are in English in an Italian draft, which the sentence count does not see.
- Time: median 15.7 s, 95th percentile 20.6 s, maximum 21.1 s, within the 90 s budget. No run ends near the writing limit (four did at 6fc5daab).
- Claim check (3 sets, 1 run each, the same 263 items): 0 false keeps and 0 errors; 6 false drops (5 in the adversarial set, 1 in the held-out set, 0 in the frozen set), the same 6 as before, all of a language.
- A person read all 31 final drafts (`verdicts.md` of the evaluation folder): 11 accepted (the 6 descriptions with no role, which stay empty, and `example-en`, `en-sparse`, `it-sparse`, `fr-sparse`, `pt-sparse`), 20 rejected. The rejected drafts show technologies that are soft skills or no tools (`example-it`, `example-de`, `example-fr`, `example-pt`, `es-sparse`, `it-plain`, `pt-plain`, `pt-traps`, `de-sparse`), a first-person quote followed by the same duty in the third person (`en-plain`, `de-plain`, `es-plain`, `es-traps`, `fr-plain`, `fr-traps`, `pt-plain`), and a nonsense phrase in a summary (`de-traps`).

The thresholds are not met: the summary and the role descriptions are short of 90%, and 20 of the 31 drafts have a defect a reader would see. The facts hold (0 invented by the counter) and the writing no longer ends at the limit, but the draft is not yet the complete, credible CV the aim asks for. The maintainer's try on the release candidate is the human check before submission.

**Measured on macOS 26: streamed answers are too slow there** (macOS 26.6.2 on a MacBook Pro, Xcode 27.0, revision 80f56370). On 26 the framework streams a structured answer about 3 times slower than it answers it whole: streamed, every draft failed its first pass at about 96 s (`en-plain` took 26 s answered whole), and the section requests failed their 30 s. So the draft streams its requests only on iOS 27 and macOS 27 (`FoundationModelsService.streamsAnswers`); on 26 the first pass and every section request ask for the whole answer, as the first pass did at 26fbf858. The cost on 26: a first pass that loops ends at its 90 s limit instead of being stopped by `RepeatingAnswer`, and a section answer cut by its token cap fails as a request instead of keeping its whole parts. Answered whole, the section requests of the first inputs took 4 to 18 s each, within their 30 s, and the drafts 2 to 3 minutes: over the 90 s budget, which is set on iOS 27. The whole set at 0775df4a (37 inputs, 1 run each, against 26fbf858 on the same Mac): 0 invented facts; 10 failed runs against 9, the same nine plus `jobs-pt`, whose first pass ran past its 90 s; a median of 119 s and a 95th percentile of 196 s (26fbf858: 116 s and 216 s); a summary in 14 of 23 distinct drafts (10 of 24), a description on 25 of 45 kept jobs (23 of 49), and enough tools of the trade and soft skills in 10 of 23 (9 of 24). So on 26 the draft is no worse than before, and thinner and slower than on 27 (#359).
