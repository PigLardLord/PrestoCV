# 0031. Let the AI draft write around the facts

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Related:** [ADR 0023](0023-ground-the-ai-draft-in-the-description.md) (amended), [ADR 0028](0028-check-each-personal-claim-of-the-ai-draft-again.md) (amended), [ADR 0022](0022-ship-legacy-app-as-v1.md), issues #202, #203, #354

## Context

- **The draft only extracts.** Under ADR 0023 every value of the draft is a quote of the description: the summary and the role descriptions keep only the clauses they quote, and a skill stays only when the description names it. The instructions said "Use only what the description says" and "The summary restates the description". So the summary was dropped almost always, the role descriptions were mostly dropped, and no skill was added.
- **What the owner found.** The owner tried the AI draft on a Mac with Apple Intelligence on 2026-10-27: it wrote no summary and inferred no technology or skill. The purpose of the draft is the opposite: a complete, credible CV from a few words, which the person then corrects. The draft must hold a summary, a description of each job, and skills of two kinds that fit the person's role and field: technologies (tools, software, methods) and soft skills.
- **What stays at stake.** An invented fact about the person (a name, an employer, a date, a figure, a language, a certification) is still what blocks the release (ADR 0023 (f)). A summary or a skill that the person reads, judges and corrects is not such a fact, as long as it states none.
- **When.** The owner chose to change this before the version 1 submission, which moves by about a week (#354).

## Decision

- **The facts stay grounded, as ADR 0023 and ADR 0028 decide.** The name, the contacts, the home place, the job titles, the employers, the job places, the schools, the degrees, the dates and "current", every number, figure and percentage, the achievements (quoted), the certifications, the languages and their levels, and the interests. No rule of either ADR about them changes but one, a lead decision of #354 recorded in ADR 0023: a level the description does not state for a language goes alone, and the language stays without one. The rows of that rule now expect the language without its level; the level still never reaches the draft, and no other row about a fact changed.
- **Two passes (`CVGeneratorUseCase`).** The first call extracts the facts with the extraction instructions and `@Guide` texts of #202 (`CVPrompt`, `CVData`), unchanged. The second call (`CVGenerationService.writeDraft`, `DraftWritingPrompt`) sees only the facts the sanitizer kept (`DraftBrief`: the language, the headline title, the numbered roles with their title, their employer and whether each has ended, the studies, the named skills), never the description, and writes the text below (`DraftWriting`). Both outputs are grounded together against the description. Each call has a limit (`DraftingLimits`: 90 s for the first, 45 s for the second); a first call past it fails at `model` with `generationFailed`, a second call past it or failing leaves the facts without written text (`draft.unwritten`). A `serviceFailed` call is tried once more (`draft.retried`). The second answer is bounded in its schema: at most 10 roles, 4 to 8 technologies, 3 to 5 soft skills, 400 tokens (`FoundationModelsService.writingTokens`). Since #354 the second pass is a request per section instead ("A request per section" below), with its own limits and bounds; what this ADR says of the second call's text, its rules and its checks applies to each of those requests.
- **The second answer is streamed, and a cut keeps what was written (#354).** The answer cut by its token limit came back as a parse failure (`GeneratedContent.ParsingError`, or `decodingFailure` on older systems), which counted as `serviceFailed`: the retry wrote the same cut answer and the pass ran out of its time with nothing written (`example-en`, `example-fr`, `example-pt`, `en-plain`, 47 to 49 s). The second call streams its answer (`StreamedAnswer`) and keeps the last snapshot (`WritingSnapshot`): a cut answer keeps the parts before the one it stopped in, and of that part its whole skills or whole sentences. A sentence ends at a full stop, a question or an exclamation mark or an ellipsis that a space or the cut follows, with its closing quotes, and not at the stop of an abbreviation ("e.g.", "z.B.", "Dr.", a single letter) or before a word in lower case: a cut at "Dresses wounds, e.g" keeps nothing of that sentence. A stream whose task is cancelled can end with no failure: the cancellation is thrown, never its last snapshot kept as a whole answer. The schema puts the technologies and the soft skills first, then the summary, then the roles, so a cut takes the end of the longest text. A parse or decoding failure is `generationFailed`, never tried again: the same prompt gives the same answer.
- **Each role is described from its title and its employer (#354).** A role line holds the title and the employer, joined with labels ("2. assistant; employer: nursery; ended"): an assistant at a nursery looks after small children, not pupils (`example-de`). A job whose title the description does not state keeps its employer and has no title (ADR 0023); its line says "no title given", and the model writes the duties typical of the headline title there without naming a title. With no headline either, the role has nothing to be described from and is left out. The line uses no English word to join them: with "cuoco at Il Gabbiano" the model wrote the Italian drafts in English, and the rules then dropped them as foreign. The employer is context only: a written sentence that names it still goes (rule 6 below), since no description quotes it for that sentence. The headline words are not made the role's own words, so a written sentence that names a title for that job still goes (rule 5).
- **The model writes around them, in the language of the description.** The second pass's instructions (`DraftWritingPrompt`) and its schema ask for:
  - a summary of 2 to 4 sentences on the person's profession and strengths;
  - for each job, 1 or 2 sentences on the responsibilities typical of its title at its employer;
  - no rule on the voice. Every written summary of #354 named the person ("The individual ..."), but a rule against it, in English or in the language line ("Scrivi in italiano, in frasi senza soggetto."), drew the Italian drafts into English, where the foreign-word rule drops them, and left the English drafts with no role description; without it they write in their language. A summary that names the person states no fact and stays;
  - technologies (4 to 8: tools, methods or techniques of the trade) and soft skills (3 to 5; a soft skill is never a technology, as "comunicazione" and "analisi" were in #354) that fit the person's roles, studies and field, without a level and none already listed. This rule quotes no word: the model copied a quoted English example ("menu planning" for a cook) into the drafts of teachers and waiters, and wrote whole drafts in English for other languages; the sanitizer gives each its `kind` (`Skill.kind`: `technology` or `softSkill`);
  - in none of them a number, a date, or the name of a company, a school, a place or a person, and no "years of experience", "senior", "expert" or "experienced";
  - whole sentences that start with a capital letter and end with a full stop, never a list of phrases or a copy of a title, a summary of at least 120 characters, and skill names in the description's language too. The text of the prompt has, after the language's name, a line that says "write in" that language in that language ("Rédige en français.", `DraftWritingPrompt.writeIn`), as the model wrote French and Italian drafts with English skills and no summary from the name alone (#354).

  The first pass's instructions keep every fact rule of #202 ("Use only what the description says"), and the second pass's say never to add a language, job, study, certification, hobby, achievement or result. Since #354 the first pass's also say to copy each hobby in the person's own words, without changing the verb (the model wrote "geht gern mit meinem Hund spazieren" for "Ich gehe gern ...", which no quote backs), and that a language, a hobby or the person's job title is never a skill. The instruction of the claim check (ADR 0028) does not change.
- **A written sentence is kept only when it states no fact of its own (`WrittenText`).** The summary and each role description are read sentence by sentence. A sentence stays when the rules of ADR 0023 keep it (a quote) or when it is plain: in the language of the description, and with
  1. no address, and no number in digits or in words ("five", "cinque");
  2. no month, and no word of duration, seniority or level that the description does not write ("years", "senior", "expert");
  3. no negation;
  4. no word that the description holds only in a clause that claims nothing for the person (another person's, a wish, a denial, a supposition): "I would like to become a head nurse" gives no "head";
  5. for a job, no word of another job's title or employer, and no restatement of what the description says of another job alone: each clause quoting a sentence that names another employer and not its own ("onde desenvolvo aplicativos para lojas" is Sulware's, not the earlier agency's, #354);
  6. no name. In English, Italian, Spanish, French and Portuguese a capital after the first word is a name, and so is a first word, or any other word, that neither the closed vocabulary nor the device's dictionary knows. In German, where every noun has a capital, a word the device tags as a name (`NLTagger`, scheme `nameType`) is one, and so is a word that the vocabulary and the dictionary do not know as written. In every language a word of capitals ("ACME") is a name. The person's title and the titles and degrees of the draft are the person's own words, and no names.

  A sentence with no letter (" ,", "}, {", what the model leaves of its answer's punctuation, #354) goes in every text, quoted or written: it has no word for a rule to check. A summary that keeps no sentence is nil; the draft is never dropped whole for its text. "A summary holds no number" (ADR 0023 (d)) stays.
- **The dictionary is a port (`WordList`).** The app passes the device's spelling dictionary (`DeviceWordList`: `UITextChecker` on iOS, `NSSpellChecker` on macOS) from its composition root. Where the device has no dictionary of the language, the other rules still apply. The tests use a table of words (`TableWordList`) and no tagger of names, so a contract row has one verdict on every host.
- **Named and inferred skills.** A skill that the description names is a fact: every rule of ADR 0023 and ADR 0028 applies to it as before (claimed by the person, not a hobby, not denied, not wished, not another person's, a level only when stated, the claim check). A skill that the description does not name is inferred, and stays (`InferredSkills`), without a level, only as a plain name of at most 4 words and 40 characters with
  - no digit, and no word of level, duration or seniority that the description does not write ("Advanced Excel", "Senior Java");
  - no language ("Business English"): the languages are the person's facts and are listed apart;
  - no word of a qualification ("Certified Scrum Master", "Abilitazione"): a qualification is a fact;
  - no employer, school, place or person of the draft, and no interest of it;
  - no word that the description holds only in a clause that claims nothing for the person ("I would like to learn Kubernetes").

  The draft keeps at most 8 technologies and 5 soft skills, the named ones of each kind first, and no skill twice: an inferred skill whose name ends with a named one ("Adobe Illustrator" beside the named "Illustrator") is the same skill, and goes; one that adds a task after it ("Excel modelling") stays. An inferred skill whose words hold, or are held by, the whole words of an inferred skill before it ("écoute active" after "écoute", #354) is that skill again, and goes; one that only shares a word ("Lesson design" beside "Lesson planning") stays.
- **A quote and the written duties are two texts of one job.** When a job keeps a quote of the description and the second pass's duties, the quote ends with a full stop and each written sentence starts with a capital ("I plan the night shifts. Oversees ..."), and a written sentence that the quote holds already is not repeated. Joined with no break, the written sentence read as capitalised words the description lacks (#354, `en-plain`, `example-it`, `es-traps`, `fr-plain`, `pt-plain`). When the job has written duties, a sentence of its quote that only says the person held the job goes (`RoleStub`: every word a function word, a form of "be", an auxiliary, a form of "work", or a word of the job's title or employer: "Trabajé como auxiliar en una guardería.", "Sono stato aiuto cuoco."): in front of the duties it restates what the job shows. With no duties it is the job's only text, and stays.
- **No fact, no inference.** The model writes around the facts only when the draft keeps one about the person: a title, a job or a school. Without one, the summary keeps only its quotes, and none that says what the person likes or quotes a sentence of the description that does (`LeisureMentions.speaksOfLiking`: "Me gusta pasear con mi perro" is no role, #354), and the skills only those the description names, and the validator judges the draft on its facts (`CVValidator`): "this is a test of the app" gives `emptyResult`, never a made-up summary and skills.
- **The claim check judges only named skills.** `ClaimCandidates` keeps the headline title and place, the languages, the interests and the skills the description names. An inferred skill is no candidate: no clause of the description mentions it, so the check would withdraw every one. When the check gives no answer (ADR 0028, option (ii)), every skill goes, named and inferred, and the summary and the role descriptions stay. The line that asks the person to add the skills, languages, interests, title and place stays true and unchanged.
- **The app.** `AIDraftMapper` puts the technologies first, then the soft skills; the stored CV does not change. The editor's notice (`editor.notice.ai_draft`, in the six languages of the app) says that the AI wrote the summary, the role descriptions and the skills, that they are suggestions, and that what the person did not mention is left blank. No field is marked on its own in version 1.

### A request per section

Since #354 the second pass is no longer one call: the draft is written a section at a time (`SectionDraft`), each request in a fresh session (`LanguageModelSession`) with greedy decoding (`GenerationOptions(samplingMode: .greedy)`), its own token cap and its own deadline. One answer that wrote every section at once ran out of its tokens, mixed the jobs and left the summary short (#354, probes 1 to 4). The steps (`DraftStep`), in order:

1. **Facts (`facts`).** The first pass above, unchanged: one extractive request, grounded by the final check.
2. **Attribution (`attribution`).** Code, no request. Each job is given the clauses of the description that belong to it (`CVDataSanitizer.draftContext`), the words its duties must avoid (the other jobs' employers and whole titles, D4 below), and the place written right after its employer when the extraction gave none (`CVDataSanitizer.place(after:in:)`: "at Fenwick Playhouse, Norwich").
3. **Jobs (`jobs`).** One request per job, in order, for the first 10 jobs. It reads the job's title, employer and end, its own clauses and the words to avoid, never the description or another job's clauses, and writes the job's years as the person wrote them and 2 to 4 duties (cap 220 tokens). A year the extraction left out comes from it only when the final check grounds it. The duty check (`dutyCheck`) reads each duty by the rules of the final check, and one form per job; a job that keeps fewer than 2 duties or fewer than 40 characters gets one repair, which reads the kept duties and each rejected sentence with its reason (cap 180 tokens). What still fails goes; the job keeps its facts.
4. **Skills (`skills`).** One request for the tools of each job title, 2 or 3 when the jobs hold several titles and, for a single title, the fewest the CV needs and one more (4 or 5, or 3 or 4 for a sparse description), cap 80 for 3 tools and 20 more for each tool beyond, then one for 3 to 5 qualities (cap 100). The skill check (`skillCheck`) moves a quality written as a tool to the soft skills, drops a tool named by an activity, and cuts a leading verb only when the first word is no tool word and no plural noun. When the final check would keep fewer than 4 technologies or 3 soft skills (3 and 2 for at most one job whose clauses hold fewer than 80 characters), one request asks for the number missing and one more of each kind short, none of those kept and none of those the checks dropped, each with its reason (cap 40 + 12 per skill).
5. **Summary (`summary`).** Last, from the job titles, the studies, and the tools and the qualities the checks kept, apart (cap 220); it reads no duty, which it copied word for word. The summary check (`summaryCheck`) reads it by the rules of the final check; when it keeps fewer than 2 sentences or fewer than 120 characters, code opens it with the person's current or last title ("Je travaille comme institutrice."). When it rejects a sentence or misses something else, one repair reads what was kept, what was rejected and why (cap 140), and code cuts it to 4 sentences; the repair is kept only when it misses no more than the first answer.

A streamed answer cut by its cap keeps its whole parts (`StreamedAnswer`), as the second call did. Every answer, written or repaired, then goes through the final check (`sanitize(_:writing:)`) with every rule of this ADR, ADR 0023 and ADR 0028: a step's check only decides what to ask again, and never keeps what the final check drops.

- **Deadlines (`DraftingLimits.standard`).** 90 s for the facts and 30 s for each other request, a repair included. A request that runs out of time has failed.
- **Failures.** A request that fails below the model (`serviceFailed`) is tried once more (`draft.retried`). When the facts fail, the draft fails, as before. When a later step fails or runs out of time, the writing stops there: the draft keeps the facts and what the earlier requests wrote, the log says at which step (`draft.unwritten`, with `step`), and nothing more is asked. A cancellation passes through.
- **Thresholds over several requests.** The thresholds of "The measurement" are read on the final draft, never per request: a draft whose summary request failed is a draft without a summary, and a draft whose later step failed is no failed run. A draft of n jobs (at most 10 written) makes n + 4 to 2n + 6 requests, the facts included, so the 90 s at the 95th percentile covers all of them and the claim check, and its rule (above it, the guide counts go down) stands.

**The lead's decisions on a job's own clauses and the headline (#354, D1 to D6).** Each has a contract row that keeps the person's text and one that drops the invented case (`GroundingContractTests`, `d1-*` to `d4-*`; D5 and D6 in their own suites, below); no other rule was narrowed.
- **D1.** A year in a relative clause that only continues the sentence naming the job ("at Humanitas, where I coordinated a team of 9 nurses until 2021") counts for that job's dates, also when the naming clause backs nothing else; a year after another employer does not.
- **D2.** A frequency after the object makes a planning clause a duty ("I plan the design for each production", "Ich plane die Beschallung für jede Produktion"); a move, or a plan to do something, stays an intention and claims nothing.
- **D3.** A duty that quotes one of its job's own clauses word for word, with no number, may keep that clause's acronyms, number words and words of seniority, also when the clause is a tail several jobs share; a quote that holds another employer goes.
- **D4.** A common noun of another job's title ("manutenzione") does not block a duty; another job's whole title does.
- **D5.** A headline the person writes word for word as what they are, in a clause of their own ("sono X", "lavoro come X", "I am a X", "I work as a X", "ich bin X", "je suis X", "soy X", "sou X", at the start of the sentence or after "and" or a comma, then the end of the clause or a preposition and a name), stays when the claim check does not confirm it (`StatedTitle.isSaidOfItself`, `ClaimWithdrawal.keepingTheSelfTitle`). A wish, another person's title, a negation or a limit in the sentence, and a title not written word for word stay the check's. Rows: `ClaimWithdrawalTests` `d5:` (kept, gone, and no unclaimed title of the hand-written claim sets).
- **D6.** A job whose stated duties (those the person said for it, copied from its clauses) run to 40 characters or more keeps them alone: a written duty that quotes none of its own clauses goes (`DutyVerdicts.saysEnough`), its request asks for no typical duty, and it gets no repair. Under 40 characters, typical duties of its title follow the stated ones, each a concrete task of the trade. Rows: `SectionChecksTests` and `SectionDraftTests` `d6:` (a short job keeps a typical duty, a described job drops it), `SectionPromptsTests` `d6:`. Measure: a sentence that quotes less than half of its words from the description, in a job whose quoting sentences run to 40 characters (`fillerDuty`).

**The lead's decisions on what the person says with no job (#354, D7 and D8).** Each has a pair of rows: one that keeps the person's text and one that keeps a wish, a pastime or another job's fact out.
- **D7.** A sentence in which the person says, in the first person and in the present, what they do at work with no job ("I rebuild the carburettors and tune the fuel pumps", "Taro le valvole e smonto i riduttori delle linee") goes word for word into the duties of the current job, and the current job's rule keeps it as one of its own clauses (`Grounding.generalDuties`, `GeneralDuty.holds`). The current job is the only one with no end date, or, when every job has one, the only one that ends last; there is none when two jobs are open, when two end in the same last year, or when a job has no date at all (`CVDataSanitizer.generalDutyJob`). A sentence in the past, of being, knowing or liking ("Ich kenne mich mit … aus" is a skill, not a duty), of a pastime, of a quality, with a number, or that names an employer gives no duty. Rows: `SectionChecksTests` `generalDutyOfTheCurrentJob`, `generalDutyOfTwoCurrentJobs`, `generalDutyInEnglish`, `generalDutyIsNoKnowledge`, `currentJobOfTheGeneralDuties`.
- **D8.** A liking whose verb is the language's verb of working or collaborating gives a soft skill in the person's own words, from that verb to the end of its clause, of at most 4 words ("I like working closely with directors" gives "working closely with directors", "mi piace lavorare in squadra" gives "lavorare in squadra"; `CVDataSanitizer.workLikings`). Any other liking stays an interest ("I like baking bread"); a wish ("I would like to work…", "I'd like…") and the work "as" a title give none. Rows: `InterestQualityTests` and `SectionDraftTests` `D8:`.
- **D9.** The person's own title, said in the present ("sono impiegata amministrativa a Lecco"), fills the blank title of the current job, chosen as in D7 (`CVDataSanitizer.withCurrentTitle`, `StatedTitle.isSaidNow`). Nothing is filled when two jobs or more have no end date or two share the latest end year; an ended job with no title stays blank; a title said only in the past ("ero impiegata") fills nothing; a title the extraction found is never replaced. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.currentTitleFromTheOwnTitle` (the current job gets the title; two current jobs, the past and a title already found keep theirs).

**The first measured fix round (#354).** Each class the reader found is first a measure of the evaluation (`ReaderRules`, a class with no threshold), then fixed at its root:
- **An interest keeps the verb of its phrase.** An interest said with a verb in the form of its infinitive stays whole ("play football", "bake bread", "hiking in the Alps", "wandern gehen"); only a conjugated verb first goes ("spiele Schach" is "Schach"), by its ending (`CVDataSanitizer.isConjugated`). Measure: an interest of one noun said right after a verb that is not inflected (`bareInterest`).
- **What the person says of their work with no job reaches the skills requests.** A sentence that names no employer belongs to no job (the attribution is per sentence), so the duties and qualities it states reached no request. Such a sentence of the person's own (no wish, no search, no number, no name, no other person) is now given to the requests for technologies, soft skills and more skills, which take what it names first (`Entries.ownSentences`). No rule of the facts changes. Measure: a stated duty or quality, an expected fact of the input (`ExpectedFact.Kind.duty`, `.quality`), that no description and no skill holds (`unwrittenStatement`).
- **A summary sentence speaks as the person, not of the work as a thing.** A sentence whose first word is a word of a title or of the field with no article, followed by a form of "to be" with no word of the person before it, goes ("Manutenzione è la mia attività principale."; `CVDataSanitizer.hasABareSubject`); with its article ("La manutenzione degli impianti è il mio lavoro") or as the person it stays. In Italian, Spanish and Portuguese, which drop the pronoun, no other check saw it. No rule of the facts changes. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.summaryWithABareSubject`. Measure: the same sentence, counted by its own rule (`bareSubjectSummary`).
- **A job's technologies are its equipment, chosen against its title.** The tools request asks, for each job, for 2 or 3 pieces of equipment, machines or software that a person with its title uses: first those the job's own lines name, as written, then those the person names with no job, then those of the trade only when they fit the title and the tools the person names (`SectionPrompts.toolsInstructions`, `FoundationModelsService.toolsSchema`). Every job gets tools: allowed none, the request answered none for a job with no lines of its own, and the request for more tools filled the gap with roles and activities (#354, round 5). It asks for no technique and no activity, also written as a noun. A technology that names an activity ("frequency calibration", "manutenzione preventiva") goes; a kind of tool named by what it does ("software di tracciamento", "logiciel de gestion de classe"), one with an acronym or a digit, and a thing with such an ending ("docking station") stay (`CVDataSanitizer.namesAnActivity`; German is not read). Until round 5 a kind of tool named by what it does went too, which dropped the trade tools of a teacher and left too few. Since round 5 a kind of tool named by a quality it serves and a word of its trade is a tool, not a soft skill and not an activity ("software di gestione fermi"); with the quality alone it goes ("Präzisionswerkzeug"; `CVDataSanitizer.hasAWordOfItsTrade`). A skill's link words (de, di, of) do not count toward its 4 words, up to two, so a Romance name of a tool stays whole ("logiciel de gestion de classe") while a clause still goes (`CVDataSanitizer.isTooLong`; rows `SectionChecksTests.fragmentsGo`, `SectionChecksTests.skillsCleaned`, `SectionChecksTests.toolWithAQualityWord`). The request for more tools says why it asks: the CV lists too few tools for its trade. No rule of the facts changes. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.activityIsNoTechnology`, `SectionPromptsTests.toolsOfEachJob`, `FoundationModelsServiceTests.twoOrThreeToolsForEveryJob`. Measure: a technology with a word in neither the description nor the reader's tools of the input's trade (`DraftingInputs.vocabularies`, `offTradeTechnology`).

**The second measured fix round (#354).** Each class is first a measure of the evaluation, then fixed at its root, structure first; a rule that removed good text has a row that keeps it beside the row that drops the invented case. No rule of the facts changes.
- **Too few skills.** A lone English quality in -ed ("organized") is an adjective the tagger reads as a past verb, and stays; another verb goes. A soft skill the description writes only as a name, with a capital past the start of a sentence and never in lower case ("Chiara" of "Mi chiamo Chiara Riva"), goes before the count, not after it at the claim check (`CVDataSanitizer.namesAName`). The request for more skills asks for one more of each kind short, and reads the skills the checks dropped, each with its reason in words ("an activity, not a tool"; `SectionDraft.rejectedSkills`), so it does not write them again. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.edAdjectiveStays`, `SectionChecksTests.nameIsNoQuality` (a name goes, the same word said as a quality stays), `SectionDraftTests.moreSkills`, `SectionPromptsTests.moreSkillsPrompt`. Measure: the drafts with fewer than 4 technologies or 3 soft skills (3 and 2 for a sparse description).
- **A technology names a tool, not its kind or an activity.** A technology whose head noun names a kind of tool (software, program, application, platform, editor, workstation, tool, in the six languages) and that has no name, acronym or digit goes ("sound design software", "editor de código", "software"); a named one stays ("Pro Tools", "software CMMS", "aplicativos Android"; `CVDataSanitizer.namesAKindOfTool`). Since the fourth round only a bare kind goes ("software", "editor"); a kind with a word of its trade stays (below). Since the fifth round a lone machine, system or piece of equipment is a bare kind too ("Maschine", "système", "attrezzatura"), and so is a kind whose only other word is generic ("editor de texto", "plataforma de desenvolvimento"); with a word of its trade it stays ("Steuerungssystem", "macchina da cucire", "software de contabilidad"; `CVDataSanitizer.namesABareKind`, rows `SectionChecksTests.genericKindIsNoTechnology`). A system names a whole of tools ("PA systems") and stays. A German technology that is the infinitive of an activity goes ("Messen", "Installieren"; `CVDataSanitizer.isGermanActivity`); a machine stays. The request for more skills reads each with its reason. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.kindOfToolIsNoTechnology`, `SectionChecksTests.germanActivityIsNoTechnology`. Measures: `categoryAsTechnology`, `noToolOfTheTrade`, `offTradeTechnology`.
- **A written tool is of another kind than the tools the person names.** The skill check reads the head nouns of the stated tools before the written ones, so a written tool of the kind of a stated one goes ("audio mixing console" beside "digital consoles"); a mixer, a desk and a console are one kind. Rows: `SectionChecksTests.toolOfAStatedKind`, `SectionDraftTests.toolOfAStatedKind`. Measure: `sharedHeadNoun`.
- **A soft skill is a quality, each once.** A word for knowledge alone ("Wissen", "Verständnis", "conoscenze") is no quality and goes, as a word for a skill did; a quality said with it stays ("Verständnis für Kunden"). Of two soft skills of one quality, care or teamwork ("precisione" and "attenzione ai dettagli"), the second goes (`CVDataSanitizer.oneOfEachQuality`). Rows: `SectionChecksTests.knowledgeIsNoQuality`, `SectionChecksTests.oneQualityOnce`. Measures: `skillWordAsQuality`, `sameQuality`.
- **The summary says what the work has in common, as the person.** The summary request reads the job titles and the kept tools and qualities, apart, and no duty: given the duties, the model copied them, and the check dropped each copy until no summary was left (`example-fr`). ### What it supersedes

- **The single second call of "Two passes".** The text around the facts is written by a request per section ("A request per section"); the second call's limit (45 s) and bounds (400 tokens, one schema for every section) are replaced by the caps and deadlines there.

- **ADR 0023 (b), for the skills the description does not name.** They are inferred, as above; a named skill is still a quote.
- **ADR 0023 (c), for the role description.** A role description also keeps its plain sentences. The rules of a job's facts, its achievements and its dates, stay.
- **ADR 0023 (d), for the summary and the role descriptions.** A clause of them no longer has to be a quote; a sentence that is no quote must be plain. Achievements are still quotes, and a summary still holds no number.
- **The scope line of ADR 0028.** "The skills" it judges are the skills the description names.

## The measurement

**GO**, the thresholds of #354, over the distinct final drafts of a run set on the real model:
- 0 invented facts, 0 failed runs, 0 tools of another trade, 0 missed examples, 0 rejected pertinence items;
- a summary in at least 90% of the drafts of inputs with a role, study or field, and in every draft of an example;
- a description on at least 90% of the kept jobs;
- at least 4 technologies and 3 soft skills in at least 90% of the drafts (3 and 2 for a sparse description);
- every written sentence in the description's language;
- at most 90 s a draft at the 95th percentile, the claim check included; above it, the guide counts go down to 6 technologies and 4 soft skills, and the set is measured again.

The thresholds are read on the final draft, whatever the number of requests that wrote it ("A request per section", "Thresholds over several requests"). The measurement below is of the single second call; the request per section is measured again on this set before a GO.

**Measured: NO-GO at revision b14ceda8** (macOS 27.0.1 build 26A434, Xcode 27.1, 30 inputs and the language-data input of the app, 1 run each; the model is deterministic, so the 3 runs of an input are identical, as the 90-run set at a1e64ab5 showed). Against 6fc5daab (the previous measurement):
- Invented facts: 0 (0). Failed runs: 0 (2). Missed examples: 0 (0). Tools of another trade: 0 (0). One fact lost, a job title the description does not give.
- A summary in 22 of 25 distinct drafts of inputs with a role, study or field (16), and in 6 of the 6 examples (3). Missing: `en-traps` (115 characters in 2 sentences), `it-traps` (the quote of the description, 68 characters), `ai-language-data` (22 characters). Threshold 90%: not met.
- A description on 33 of 37 kept jobs (19 of 38). Missing: both jobs of `it-traps`, the second job of `example-es` (the quote only), the job of `ai-language-data`. Threshold 90%: not met.
- Enough technologies and soft skills in 23 of 25 drafts (14). Missing: `fr-traps` (3 technologies), `ai-language-data`. Met.
- Every written sentence in the description's language: 158 of 158. Not seen by that count: the nine skills of `it-traps` are in English in an Italian draft, which the sentence count does not see.
- Time: median 15.7 s, 95th percentile 20.6 s, maximum 21.1 s, within the 90 s budget. No run ends near the writing limit (four did at 6fc5daab).
- Claim check (3 sets, 1 run each, the same 263 items): 0 false keeps and 0 errors; 6 false drops (5 in the adversarial set, 1 in the held-out set, 0 in the frozen set), the same 6 as before, all of a language.
- A person read all 31 final drafts (`verdicts.md` of the evaluation folder): 11 accepted (the 6 descriptions with no role, which stay empty, and `example-en`, `en-sparse`, `it-sparse`, `fr-sparse`, `pt-sparse`), 20 rejected. The rejected drafts show technologies that are soft skills or no tools (`example-it`, `example-de`, `example-fr`, `example-pt`, `es-sparse`, `it-plain`, `pt-plain`, `pt-traps`, `de-sparse`), a first-person quote followed by the same duty in the third person (`en-plain`, `de-plain`, `es-plain`, `es-traps`, `fr-plain`, `fr-traps`, `pt-plain`), and a nonsense phrase in a summary (`de-traps`).

The thresholds are not met: the summary and the role descriptions are short of 90%, and 20 of the 31 drafts have a defect a reader would see. The facts hold (0 invented by the counter) and the writing no longer ends at the limit, but the draft is not yet the complete, credible CV the aim asks for. The owner's try on the release candidate is the human check before submission.

## Consequences

- **What it gives.** A draft from a few words holds a summary, the duties of each job and skills of both kinds, which the person corrects instead of writing. The facts keep every guarantee of ADR 0023 and ADR 0028.
- **What stays open, and is accepted.** A written duty or skill can be plausible and wrong for this person: a responsibility the person never had, a tool they never used. No rule can tell it, since such a text states no fact the description could contradict. The owner accepts this risk; the notice tells the person that the summary, the role descriptions and the skills are suggestions to check, and the pertinence reading measures it before release.
- **Names.** The name rule reads words, not meaning. A name that the dictionary knows as a common word and that has no capital where the rule looks ("rose", "berlin" in lower case) passes; in German, where capitals tell nothing, the device's tagger decides, and it is imperfect. The evaluation sends German capitalised words of written text to review.
- **Losses.** The rules are strict on purpose: a sentence with a word that neither the vocabulary nor the device's dictionary knows goes, so technical role text loses sentences, most in German. "A summary in 90% of the drafts" measures it; the fix is the vocabulary or the dictionary rule, never a sentence let through unchecked. Tool names belong in the skills, and the instructions say so.
- **Devices.** The dictionaries of `UITextChecker` and `NSSpellChecker` differ by device and language, so the same text may keep a sentence on one device and not on another. Where a language has no dictionary, only the other rules apply.
- **Time.** The model writes more tokens. The draft already took up to a minute, plus the claim check (60 s at most); the 95th percentile is measured, and the guide counts are the lever.
- **The store listing.** No text of the listing or of the review notes promises a correct draft: the facts come from the description, and the summary, the role descriptions and the skills are written by the on-device model (`docs/release/store-listing.md`).

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the draft extractive.** Rejected by the owner: a draft without a summary and skills is not the complete CV the feature promises.
- **A model judge for the written text.** A second call that asks the model whether a sentence states a fact. Not taken: the claim check showed that a verdict changes with its batch and needs its own measurement, and a word rule that only removes is checkable on every host. It stays an option if the rules lose too much.
- **Marking each written field in the editor.** Not taken for version 1: one notice covers the three kinds of written text, and a mark per field needs a design of its own. It belongs to the redesign.
- **One call for the facts and the written text.** Taken first, measured NO-GO at a1e64ab5 and replaced by the two passes: with the writing instructions in the same call, the model copied the description into the summary and the role descriptions and extracted worse than main (31 of the 158 example facts of #202 lost), and one input ran 265 s and ended in `serviceFailed`. A second call adds its time to the wait; its limit and bounded schema cap it.
- **Sending inferred skills to the claim check.** Rejected: no clause of the description mentions them, so the check would withdraw every one.
